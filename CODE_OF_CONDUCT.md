# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people learning TypeScript, returning to an old project, or contributing experienced feedback. Help others participate without having to defend their background or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Discuss the code and its consequences rather than attacking the person who wrote it.
- Give actionable feedback, explain technical disagreements, and remain open to correction.
- Respect other people's time, privacy, and boundaries. Ask before sharing personal information.
- Keep issues, pull requests, and review comments relevant to the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. These expectations apply to project spaces and when representing this project elsewhere.

## Reporting and moderation

Report a concern to the maintainer, [@shipiyouniao](https://github.com/shipiyouniao). For a public project comment, link to the relevant interaction and explain the concern without escalating the exchange. For a sensitive matter, open an issue requesting a private contact channel and leave out personal details until that channel is established. GitHub's own reporting tools are also available for platform abuse.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior, restrict participation, or block repeat or serious offenders. Responses should reflect the context and severity, with personal details kept private as far as the available reporting channel allows. A moderation decision may be questioned respectfully through the same reporting process.
