# Mirror Twins artwork

All four project assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Bastion Guardian was used only as a style reference for chunky ivory, navy, gold and cyan dungeon artwork. The PNG files retain their generated alpha; no scripted cropping, recoloring or background removal was used. Each deliverable was generated in its own call.

## Dawn

Asset: [`public/assets/dungeon/mirror-dawn.png`](../public/assets/dungeon/mirror-dawn.png).

Prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG game boss sprite, a single Dawn Mirror Twin, no text. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: keep the chunky toy-like painted 3D fantasy dungeon style, soft studio light and readable simple silhouette, create a new character. Subject: compact mystical knight in warm ivory and amber-gold armor, rounded helmet with small sun crown, dark face and two warm glowing eyes, holding one broad polished amber mirror shield with a sun-shaped crest, short ivory cape, feet visible. Three-quarter view facing right, character centered occupying 85% of square canvas, minimal details readable at 48 pixels. Genuine transparent background/alpha, no scene, no floor, no lettering, no watermark, no frame. Single isolated complete character only.”

## Dusk

Asset: [`public/assets/dungeon/mirror-dusk.png`](../public/assets/dungeon/mirror-dusk.png).

Generation prompt, using Dawn as a family/style reference: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent PNG Dusk Mirror Twin boss sprite. Input image is a STYLE AND FAMILY REFERENCE, create a different lunar twin. Match the chunky painted 3D toy fantasy knight proportions and finish. Subject: compact knight wearing deep navy-blue and pearl-silver armor, rounded helmet crowned with a single silver crescent, dark face with two cool cyan eyes, holding broad polished moon-blue mirror shield and a short silver mace, short deep-blue cape. All feet and crown visible. Three-quarter view facing right, centered single figure filling 85 percent of a square with generous transparent margin. Genuine alpha background, no floor or scene, no text or watermark. Distinct moon silhouette, restrained details readable at 48px.”

The first result painted a checkerboard. It was rejected, then corrected through the built-in editing tool with this prompt: “Use case: background-extraction. Edit target is the supplied Dusk Mirror Twin sprite. Remove the entire baked white and gray checkerboard background and replace with TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA. Keep the knight, face, blue mirror shield, colors, proportions, position, full feet and crescent crown unchanged. Do not redraw the knight or add anything. Clean cutout with real alpha, no opaque background, no checkerboard pattern, no shadow behind it, no text. This image will be layered over game tiles.”

## Seal

Asset: [`public/assets/dungeon/mirror-seal.png`](../public/assets/dungeon/mirror-seal.png).

Prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Single isolated transparent PNG game mechanism icon: MIRROR SEAL. A squat ivory stone pedestal holding a thick round mirror medallion, left half amber sun and right half blue crescent moon, two large interlocking gold and silver arcs suggesting a lock. Chunky painted 3D toy dungeon art matching reference style only. Simple symmetric silhouette readable at 32 pixels, three quarter view, centered with 12 percent transparent margin all sides. Only the one small mechanism, no characters, no lettering, no landscape, no rectangular frame. Background must be genuinely transparent with alpha channel; do not paint a checkerboard or solid backdrop.”

## Shift portal

Asset: [`public/assets/dungeon/mirror-rift.png`](../public/assets/dungeon/mirror-rift.png).

Prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent PNG square game action icon, SHIFT REALM. A single small oval magic mirror portal made of two thick interlocking ivory, gold and silver arcs, amber light on its left, cool cyan-blue light on its right; in the center a clean curved two-way exchange arrow formed from light. Chunky painted 3D toy fantasy dungeon style matching reference style only. Very simple strong silhouette readable at 32px, centered complete object fills 80 percent of square canvas, no other objects, no character, no text, no floor, no scenery. Genuine transparent alpha channel background, not a painted checkerboard, no opaque backdrop.”
