# First chapter: the old watchtower

Status: the six-stage main route is playable. The prologue and persistent camp, northern road, quarry, lift and northwest bridge connect five exploration stages with a final guardian stage. This is the first Bastion-family arc, not the later Lookout boss. The independent [old-mine rescue](quarry-rescue.md) adds three optional minecart puzzles and Toma as a camp resident. Further side stories and later chapters remain Roadmap II work.

## Main route

Each prepared stage has three authored floors. Overworld scenes are persistent locations and do not count as additional prepared stages.

| Stage ID            | Name                   | Playable objective                                                                       | Permanent outcome                                                                     |
| ------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `tower-galleries`   | Outer galleries        | Recover supplies on three ordinary minefields and practice shared expedition tools       | Reach the inner tower route; 50 supplies once                                         |
| `tower-relay`       | An Answer in the Tower | Disconnect relay gates, rescue Nia and optionally recover an outsider record             | Nia becomes a speaking camp resident; 80 supplies once                                |
| `ridge-observatory` | Ridge Observatory      | Route power to instruments and record bearings                                           | Locate the beacon and retain its recorded sound; 100 supplies once                    |
| `old-waterway`      | Old Waterway           | Drain larger minefields and reach the beacon                                             | Hear the guardian's warning and locate the control room; 140 supplies once            |
| `tower-control`     | Control Room           | Restore the bridge supply, alarm circuit and west-line controls                          | Lower the northwest bridge; 180 supplies once                                         |
| `northwest-bastion` | Northwest Bastion      | Repair two approach floors, then disconnect two shield pylons and open the guardian core | Reopen the blockade and confirm the protagonist's arrival location; 240 supplies once |

The control room stays inside the old tower. The final stage does not reuse another doorway on the crowded old map: North Road leads west into the new bridge scene, then to the blockade approach. Crossing the bridge unlocks a physical return-to-camp shortcut. The atlas places these locations in its northwest area, without teleporting the player.

## Story promise

Lumi's lead is real: the old tower holds records about arrivals from elsewhere. Nia's rescue and the beacon search lead to a guardian still struggling to contain a damaged gate. Leyline knots have entered its circuit, so shutting off the whole system would release the gate too. The fifth stage restores individual lines; the sixth lets the player repair the guardian through the established tactical mechanics.

The guardian survives. The reopened supply route has an immediate benefit for camp, and the guardian confirms hearing the protagonist at the old western crossing on the night of their arrival. That location is the next lead, not a claim that the next region is already playable. A short camp conversation ends the current arc with the people the player has helped.

Chinese dialogue is written as short spoken exchanges. Existing chibi portraits, distinct voices and typewriter controls remain shared. The bridge lowers visibly after the accepted restoration outcome. Device and battle actions retain their animations and contextual illustrated help.

## Gameplay and save boundaries

All required floors have public-clue solution tests with the starting profession, without purchased equipment or consumable guesses. The fifth stage reaches 19 × 19; the final guardian arena is 13 × 11 because tactical movement consumes action points. See [the finale design and measured acceptance](chapter-one-finale.md) for the room table, mine density and reference routes.

Each stage retains its own journal, scene ledger, records and first-clear settlement. The shared camp still owns currency, upgrades, professions, equipment, titles and ordinary milestones. World terrain and repaired routes persist independently of failed or suspended stage attempts. First clears total 790 supplies; the final two story tasks do not add another currency payment.

Current earlier stages and world scenes retain their revisions and mine maps. New northwest scene checkpoints are additive. Unknown future envelopes remain read-only, and genuinely retired attempts continue to follow the game's existing camp-recovery policy.

The main route is complete; this does not mark the entire roadmap or every optional first-chapter activity complete.
