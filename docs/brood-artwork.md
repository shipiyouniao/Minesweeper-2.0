# Brood Queen artwork

Five original transparent PNG sprites were generated with the built-in image generation tool for the queen, defeated queen, egg, hatchling and removable web. They share the game's ivory, cobalt, brass and cyan palette. Alpha is preserved; rendering and hit testing do not depend on painted pixels.

## brood-queen

Asset: `public/assets/dungeon/brood-queen.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone square tactical Minesweeper game sprite. Subject: A regal small spider queen, rounded ivory abdomen with cobalt blue armor segments, a tiny brass crown and two turquoise gem eyes, six chunky curled legs and two small front pincers. Confident upright posture, not scary. Style: polished stylized 3D inventory illustration, soft rounded forms, minimal detail, warm ivory, cobalt blue, aged brass and subtle cyan accents. Composition: one centered object filling 78% of canvas, three-quarter view, generous margin, legible at 48px. Soft studio lighting. Truly transparent RGBA background, no floor plane, no background, no border, no lettering, no watermark, no shadow outside object. Generate one sprite only.

## brood-nest (tactics-v2)

Asset: `public/assets/dungeon/brood-nest.png`. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. This independently usable transparent sprite marks a destroyable nest; its inactive appearance is rendered with reduced opacity and grayscale. The final image was selected for consistency with the queen's ivory, cobalt, brass and cyan inventory artwork. A discarded painterly draft is not shipped.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: standalone square tactical Minesweeper game sprite. Subject: ONE compact destructible spider nest, a squat open ivory silk bowl on three cobalt blue anchor stones with small brass studs, holding three smooth pearl-like eggs with subtle cyan cracks. A clean simple hollow round silhouette, chunky toy-like forms, no branches or spikes. Style: polished stylized 3D inventory illustration, soft rounded forms, MINIMAL detail, warm ivory, cobalt blue, aged brass and subtle cyan accents. Composition: one centered object filling 75 percent of canvas, three-quarter view from above, generous clear margin, highly legible at 32px. Soft studio lighting. Truly transparent RGBA background, no floor plane, no background, no border, no lettering, no watermark, no shadow outside object. Generate exactly one sprite, no variants, no scene. Avoid painterly texture, purple colors, realistic insect eggs, brown roots, horror and busy tiny strands.

The new equipment and combat relic cards reuse the project's matching generated inventory artwork: strike for blades, guardian crests for armor, wayfarer tokens for boots, survival charms for medical supplies, scanner for calibration, salvager kit for clearing, duelist marks for the manual and chronologist dials for the hourglass. Their names and effect descriptions distinguish the gameplay choices.
