# Profession artwork and skill icons

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Project-owned PNG outputs are copied unchanged with their generated alpha channels; no downloaded artwork or placeholder glyphs are used. The existing Explorer, Surveyor and Engineer portraits retain their previous provenance in [dungeon artwork](dungeon-artwork.md).

The three new portraits serve both camp selection and walking pawns. Each of the six careers also has a separate skill icon. Images are used decoratively; localized controls and cell labels provide meaning. Inspect at 32–64 pixels and on a narrow screen.

## Exact prompts

All nine new assets are 1,254 × 1,254 PNGs. Browser pixel inspection confirmed fully transparent background pixels and a nearly opaque subject (alpha at least 250); the generated alpha was preserved unchanged. In-game screenshots: [six professions](screenshots/six-professions.png), [Sentinel skill](screenshots/sentinel-skill.png), [Archaeologist on a narrow screen](screenshots/archaeologist-mobile.png).

### archaeologist.png

Asset: `public/assets/dungeon/archaeologist.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Project asset for a minimal Minesweeper roguelite. A single centered isolated rounded 3D toy-like game icon, ivory and restrained accent colors, clean silhouette readable at 48 pixels, soft material shading, no text, no border, no floor, no scenery, no checkerboard. Actual transparent alpha PNG background. Square canvas, entire subject inside with 10 percent clear padding. Full body chibi archaeologist character, ivory face with two dark oval eyes, terracotta scarf and sand-colored safari hat, brown boots, small brush and ancient golden tablet. Friendly compact figurine, three quarter view.
