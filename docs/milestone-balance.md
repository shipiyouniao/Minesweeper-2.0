# Milestone currency balance

The earlier catalog gave 89,220 supplies across 42 one-time goals against a 39,800-supply shop. Several early claims were individually worth multiple complete expeditions. This pass lowers every mission and achievement payout while preserving all unlocks, goal thresholds, purchase prices and difficulty-scaled expedition settlement.

## Budget

| Source           |    Previous |     Current | Role                                                     |
| ---------------- | ----------: | ----------: | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 20 missions      |      15,820 |       2,190 | Small rewards while learning and unlocking tools/careers |
| 22 achievements  |      73,400 |       9,150 | Longer goals, specialist challenges and title abilities  |
| All 42 claims    |      89,220 |      11,340 | Finite supplemental income                               |
| All 26 purchases | 39,800 cost | 39,800 cost | Optional, staged build choices                           |

Total goal currency falls by **87.3%** and can cover **28.5%** of the complete shop. The [full catalog](milestones.md) gives every revised value. Already banked money is retained; already claimed rewards never pay again. Unclaimed goals use the current table, even if their counters were completed before this release. Exclusive careers, equipment, relic licenses and all 22 title abilities remain available through their existing claim IDs.

## Stage rules

- Introductory missions pay 30–100 supplies. The first skill and chest licenses each pay 60; the first boss and each family hunt pay 100.
- Intermediate missions pay 100–160. The 25-floor mission pays 250.
- Early collection achievements pay 200–350. Specialist boss achievements pay 300–400.
- Longer collection and Abyss goals pay 400–800. The largest reward is five Abyss wins at 800; it is a finite achievement, not repeatable income.
- Titles add bounded build choices rather than more money multipliers. No title increases supplies or shop purchasing power.

## Progression scenarios

These are explicit accounting scenarios, not measured player completion times. Goal requirements can overlap within a run; do not add them as if each required a separate expedition.

| Scenario                                               | Current supplies | Assumptions                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First Relaxed clear, required combat claims            |              482 | 132 minimum settlement + first boss 100 + family hunt 100 + first victory 150                                         |
| Three Relaxed wins, three different boss families      |            1,396 | 396 settlement + first boss 100 + hunts 300 + first victory 150 + five floors 100 + three bosses 150 + three wins 200 |
| Two reference Expert clears                            |            1,722 | Two chests per floor, no Treasure pouch; before goal claims                                                           |
| Two maximum Expert clears plus **every** catalog claim |           13,944 | Deliberately generous upper bound: 2 × 1,302 + 11,340; many claims cannot actually finish in two runs                 |

The last bound is below half the shop's total cost, so a fresh camp cannot buy out the shop after two Expert expeditions, even under that impossible all-goals assumption. Existing wealthy camps keep their savings.

An initial Relaxed clear still covers the Surveyor and Engineer together (100), before any goal claims. The most expensive single purchase remains the 7,500-supply archive; ten chest-free successful Abyss expeditions earn at least 7,830. Later purchases retain their staged prices. Reference settlement remains 204 / 375 / 594 / 861 / 1,431 across the five difficulties; higher difficulties keep their larger rewards.

After all goal currency is spent, 28,460 of the shop's cost remains to be funded through play. Twenty reference Abyss clears plus the entire goal catalog total 39,960; this is a budget check, not a promise that every achievement completes in twenty runs. Actual pace depends on success, chests, difficulty, prior purchases and which goals the player pursues.

## Verification and future tuning

Tests pin the complete reward budgets, retain one-time claim/ownership behavior, verify first-run role affordability, and compare the deliberately generous two-Expert bound against the real catalog total. Browser acceptance checks displayed rewards, claims and reload in English, Chinese and Japanese. Gameplay balance should next be informed by completion rate, chest collection and supplies per minute; no telemetry or human timing measurements are claimed here.

The [earlier closure audit](closure-acceptance.md#economy) records the pre-tuning economy and is retained as historical evidence. This document and the current source catalog supersede its payout values.
