# Echo Warden and expedition Sonar

## Equipment

The workshop unlocks access to the **1,600-supply Sonar license**. Equipping it costs one loadout point, starts with two pulses, and retains owned charges between floors. Twelve successful safe excavation actions restore one pulse, up to three. Flood size, repeated clicks, scans, movement and annotations do not recharge it.

A pulse costs one AP in combat. It reports the clipped 3×3 mine total and opens only the center; a mined center becomes a locked gold flag without damage. Ordinary expedition clues stay readable. Mine readings are separated by floor and Mirror realm.

## Encounter

The sixth seeded boss family has three identical, impassable resonators and one hidden core. Most numbered cells are obscured; all numbers within a scanned 3×3 region become permanently readable, including cells excavated later. Only the center opens automatically. Public green safe seams connect approaches to every resonator. The interface never marks the hidden core until published scan results uniquely locate it.

The arena lends Sonar even without ownership. Loan charges are separate from equipment, start at two, cap at three, and recharge every four safe excavations. A scan also reports whether the core is in its 3×3 region. At most two well-chosen scans can identify the core.

Approach the identified body and open its shell for one AP. Its three-turn opening permits ordinary two-AP attacks. Each third of its health is a separate phase: damage cannot skip a phase, and the core moves at the end of the turn after that health band breaks. The loan refills to at least two charges and old resonance readings expire; mine observations remain available.

Sonic rows and columns are frozen at turn start. Every third turn is quiet, allowing repositioning or attacks. Forecasts retain an immediate safe escape from the starting square; walking into another threatened dead end can still be dangerous. There are no real-time deadlines.

## Progression

- **Echo hunt:** first kill, 100 supplies.
- **Silent Footsteps:** win without losing health, 400 supplies and a title granting +1 defense at full health.
- **Perfect Pitch:** win using at most six scans, 400 supplies and a title granting +1 attack against an exposed Echo Warden.

Existing claimed achievements retain their titles and rewards. Rule revision 11 returns incompatible active expeditions to camp through the existing extraction policy.
